How to read this page

  • Forecast: red-cell units hospitals are expected to request in the next {{ horizon_days }} days.
  • Usable: units in stock minus those expiring within {{ horizon_days }} days.
  • Days of cover: how long usable stock lasts at the forecast rate.
  • Risk: HIGH under {{ horizon_days }} days of cover, MEDIUM under {% widthratio horizon_days 1 2 %} days, LOW otherwise.
  • For high-risk groups, invite eligible donors from Donor Predictions.
  • Plasma and platelets are not included: they cannot replace red cells.
